## A compact derivation

Short notes can use exactly the same equation rendering as long articles:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx=\sqrt{\pi}.
$$

You can keep a note short; there is no requirement to add metadata beyond its title, category, and ordering number.
